# Emergency plan evidence checks

Checked 26 October 2026. These checks find missing records. They do not evaluate an emergency plan, recommend an emergency response or certify compliance.

| Rule | Source and duty | What the command checks |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-GRWM-14 | [New Zealand General Risk and Workplace Management Regulations 2016, regulation 14](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2016/0013/latest/DLM6727398.html) requires a workplace emergency plan covering response, evacuation, notification, medical assistance, communication, testing and relevant worker information/training. It also requires maintenance and implementation. | Plan and procedure references, a training reference, a test record reference, the last test date, a plan-defined test interval and an owner-set review date. |
| AU-MODEL-43 | [Safe Work Australia emergency plan guidance](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/emergency-plans-and-procedures/overview) describes the model regulation 43 requirements. States and territories regulate and enforce their own duties. | The same evidence prompts. Confirm the applicable jurisdiction, additional duties and actual plan adequacy with the responsible professional. |
| LOCAL-REVIEW | Other jurisdictions need their own rule review. | Evidence gaps remain visible; no local legal rule is asserted. |

The 90-day and 180-day intervals in the seed are fictional choices in the example plans. Neither cited source specifies those universal intervals. Review dates and resource inspection dates are local controls, not legal thresholds. `readiness` says which record is missing or overdue; `compliance` identifies its source code and always calls for human review. No missing gap means only that the configured fields and dates passed.

Substantive obligations that records cannot prove include an effective response, notification at the earliest opportunity, suitable training, actual implementation and attention to workplace hazards and workforce needs. Keep approved response procedures and established emergency services in place. No notifications, evacuation decisions, public warnings, medical triage or dispatch happen here.
